Why Eidentic

Everything in one package. Fully open.

Most frameworks lead one lane — memory, or coding, or DX, or durability, or skills. Few ship all of it together, and production-readiness is usually behind an enterprise tier.

🧠

Memory that improves itself

Four-tier engine: self-editing blocks, a temporal knowledge graph, sleep-time consolidation, and passive fact extraction — not just vector recall.

🛡️

Production, built in

Durable checkpoint/resume (exactly-once), enforced cost ceilings with per-turn cost visibility, rate-limiting + quotas, OTel spans, deny-by-default permissions, sandboxed exec, secret isolation, one-call GDPR erasure, plus a batch runner and scheduled background runs.

📊

Evaluated, not vibes

A built-in eval harness with a CI pass-rate gate, and one-call promotion of a production trace into a regression test — every incident becomes a test, not a repeat.

🧩

Composable & everywhere

Ports-and-adapters: swap store, vector backend or embedder without touching agent code. Ingest PDF/HTML/Markdown; interop via MCP (with OAuth) and A2A. Apache-2.0, no code-gating. Verified on Node, Bun, and Deno in CI.
